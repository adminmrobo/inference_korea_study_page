Which seat suits me?

      
Book presentations are great for first-timers. The book is friendly, so following the range gives you a structure almost for free.

      
Paper presentations suit people who have worked with the topic in practice or want to dig deeper. Some are classics, like Transformer (2017) or Roofline (2008), which can make them a bit easier.

    

  






  
Our ground rules
Just four

  

    
Read ahead
Even if you're not presenting, skimming the week's range makes the discussion much richer.

    
Ask what you don't know
"What does this mean?" is the best question there is. Chicks are new to everything anyway.

    
Cite your sources
Note where figures, tables and numbers in your slides come from. For the book, a page or section number is fine.

    
Can't make it? Say so early
If something comes up on your presenting week, let us know in advance and we'll swap seats.

  






  
Frequently asked questions
FAQ

  

    
Where do I get the book?
You can download a free PDF from the publisher's site (baseten.co/inference-engineering) or read the online edition. The print edition is sold separately.

    
Do I need a GPU?
No. This study focuses on reading and understanding the book. If you want to run things yourself, you can start with small models using the MLX video for Apple silicon Macs (bonus video) or a free cloud notebook.

    
Can I join partway through?
Yes. Each session covers its own topic, so skim the basic terms from Session 1 (Ch. 0–1) in the Topic Guide and you'll be able to follow along.

    
Where do we meet?
All meetings are on Zoom. Meeting ID 884 5916 2312, passcode 11111. The join link and copy buttons are in How to join on the Briefing page.

    
How will I know if the time changes?
Times are still tentative. When a date changes in the sheet, the D-day, schedule and notice ticker on this board update automatically.

    
When are the special sessions (guest lectures)?
Besides the 10 regular sessions, there are special sessions · guest lectures with invited speakers. Dates and speakers are announced on Discord.

    
Where can I find past slides?
They're collected by session in the Slides folder on Google Drive.

    
Can one person do both the book and the paper presentation?
For sessions with few volunteers, yes. But we suggest sharing out the open seats first so no one person ends up carrying everything.

  






  
How does the board update?
Automatic

  
Fruit Fly ResearcherThis board reads the Google Sheet every time it opens. Just edit the sheet and presenters, open seats and dates update on their own, and once a meeting is over the egg hatches by itself. No files to edit by hand!
