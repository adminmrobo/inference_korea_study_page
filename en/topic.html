What is inference engineering?

If training is building a model, inference engineering is making that model answer people's requests fast, cheaply and accurately. Choosing GPUs, configuring inference engines, quantization and caching, deployment and autoscaling all belong here.

Training and inference

Ch. 0 · Session 1

The same model does very different jobs. Training happens rarely, as one big run. Inference happens for every request, for as long as the service is alive.

TRAINING

Training: chick school

  • Builds the weights (the model's knowledge) by looking at data
  • A few big jobs that run for weeks
  • Focused on how well it learned (quality)
→
INFERENCE

Inference: peeping for real

  • Produces an answer for each input with fixed weights
  • On every request, for many people at once
  • How fast, how cheap, how accurate
SpeedWhen does the first token appear (TTFT), and how fast do the rest follow (tokens per second)?
CostHow many customers can one GPU serve (throughput), and are any GPUs sitting idle?
QualityDid the reduced precision and shortcuts we took for speed ruin the answers?

This is where Chapter 1 of the book starts. You can't maximize all three at once, so first decide the balance that fits your service.

Pree swallows the customer's question in one gulp (prefill), and Deco answers one grain at a time (decode). Serving more customers at once is a win for the GPU as a whole, but each customer waits a bit longer. Move the sliders and press "Start peeping".
