Lab arrival log

LOG · ONE WEEK BEFORE KICKOFF

The Professor is away at a conference. Instead, a letter arrived, along with two helpers.

[SYSTEM] Dispatch notice received from the Professor’s lab. Personnel: Assistant ×1, Fruit Fly Researcher ×1. Attached: one letter, a 10-week map, one stack of glossary cards.
Professor’s Lab"Dear esteemed Study Leader! Please accept my sincere apologies for being unable to call on you in person, owing to a pressing conference engagement. Upon hearing that the chicks are to learn about inference engines, I have taken the liberty of sending our lab’s Assistant and Fruit Fly Researcher, modest though their help may be. Whether it is arranging the schedule or preparing presentations, please do not hesitate to put them to work. Ten weeks from now, on the day all ten eggs have hatched, I shall most humbly ask to hear of the results. With my enduring gratitude." — The Professor
Study LeaderWelcome aboard! This fall we’re reading Inference Engineering together for 10 weeks. Every Saturday at 8 PM: one slice of the book and one paper. Each session has one book presenter and one paper presenter.
PeepCorePeep! I gobble up tokens and peep out the next one, one at a time. But when a crowd shows up, my answers slow down. Am I hungry, or is my head just too small?
AssistantPeepCore? There’s a “core” in your name! Are you a quad-core, by any chance? Four cores, four chicks. Perfect match. Hehe.
Fruit Fly ResearcherQuad-core means CPU. PeepCore, are you secretly a Pentium? Is that “peep” just the sound of you crunching numbers?
PeepCorePeep! I’m not a CPU! I run on a GPU with thousands of cores. But no matter how many cores there are, if the feed (data) arrives slowly, they all just sit there with their beaks open, waiting. That’s the memory bandwidth story. It’s coming up in Session 3!
AssistantWhoa, so it’s the pipe, not the number of cores! It’s all in the map and glossary cards the Professor packed for us. This time I came in a lab coat instead of pajamas, so I’ll help properly.
Fruit Fly ResearcherI sorted out the order. Ch. 0 concepts → Ch. 2 models → Ch. 3 GPUs → Ch. 4 software → Ch. 5 optimization techniques → Ch. 6 modalities → Ch. 7 production. The last week is a full review. Empty presenter slots are highlighted in yellow on the schedule!
Pree · Deco · EngiPree gulps the whole question in one bite, Deco peeps out the answer one grain at a time, and Engi grabs a wrench to make it all faster! What our names mean? We’ll tell you in Session 2.
